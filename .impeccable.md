# Impeccable

## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, and fellow developers evaluating Aman — a React Native / mobile-focused software engineer. They skim quickly (often from a GitHub/X/LinkedIn link), want to see proof of craft, shipped projects, and a way to reach out. Viewed on both desktop and mobile, day and night.

### Brand Personality
Precise, confident, calm. Senior-engineer energy: restraint signals skill. No hype, no noise — the work and the polish carry the message.

### Aesthetic Direction
Soft minimal. Calm, restrained surfaces, generous whitespace, subtle motion that rewards attention without demanding it. Light and dark themes with a toggle (next-themes), both first-class — neither an afterthought. Anti-references: neon-on-dark "AI" gradients, glassmorphism cards, hero-metric templates, icon-above-heading card grids.

### Design Principles
1. Typography does the heavy lifting — distinctive display face paired with a quiet body face; few sizes, strong contrast between steps.
2. Whitespace is the layout — varied rhythm (tight groupings, generous separations), asymmetry over centered symmetry.
3. Motion is felt, not seen — one well-orchestrated load reveal; transforms/opacity only; respects reduced motion.
4. Both themes are designed, not inverted — OKLCH palette with brand-tinted neutrals tuned per theme.
5. Every element earns its place — no decoration without purpose, no cards-in-cards, no filler copy.
